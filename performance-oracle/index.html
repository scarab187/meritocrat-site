What Is the Performance Oracle?

The Performance Oracle is the proposed empirical layer of the PBS/MPS architecture, connecting a provenance-documented asset with independently observable information about how that asset performs in the real world.

What does the Performance Oracle do?

The Performance Oracle is designed to address a different question from the Provenance Ledger:

What does this asset do, and how well does it perform?

The Provenance Ledger concerns the history of the asset and its contributions. The Performance Oracle concerns observable evidence about outcomes associated with that asset.

Keeping those functions separate is important to the PBS/MPS concept because documenting who contributed to an asset is not the same thing as demonstrating how that asset performs.

Why does performance evidence matter?

Provenance can provide evidence about origin, authorship, contribution, and ownership history.

Provenance alone, however, does not demonstrate the real-world results associated with an asset.

The Performance Oracle is proposed as the layer that brings independently observable performance information into the broader PBS architecture.

Core distinction: provenance documents the history of the asset; performance evidence describes measurable outcomes associated with the asset.
Domain Examples

What can performance mean?

Performance depends on the domain being examined. Public PBS/MPS materials use different real-world measures to illustrate the concept.

Agriculture

Agricultural performance could include observable information such as crop-yield results associated with a documented asset.

Creative Works

Creative performance could involve observable economic information such as streaming revenue.

Intellectual Property

Public PBS materials identify patent citations as another example of observable performance information.

Other Measurable Domains

Public PBS materials also identify clinical outcomes as an example of domain-specific performance evidence.

Important Distinction

Does performance establish provenance?

No.

Strong performance does not establish who created an asset, who contributed to it, or who possesses legally enforceable rights associated with it.

Those questions belong to the provenance, contractual, intellectual-property, property-rights, and regulatory layers of the larger framework.

The Performance Oracle is intended to add empirical evidence, not replace the evidentiary role of provenance.

Does the Performance Oracle determine market price?

The Performance Oracle and market price are distinct concepts within the proposed architecture.

The oracle supplies performance information. The broader PBS architecture separately contemplates an Exchange Protocol concerned with listing, pricing, transfer, settlement, reporting, and auditability.

Performance evidence can therefore inform the larger system without itself constituting a market price or guarantee of economic value.

PBS Architecture

How does the Performance Oracle fit into PBS?

Layer 1

Provenance Ledger

Establishes the evidentiary history of the asset, contributions, and associated ownership information.

Layer 2

Performance Oracle

Connects the documented asset with independently observable information about real-world performance.

Layer 3

Exchange Protocol

Represents the proposed market layer for compliant listing, pricing, transfer, reporting, settlement, and auditability.

Why is independent verification important?

The public PBS/MPS concept treats independent verification as an important part of performance measurement.

The purpose is to distinguish evidence about real-world results from an issuer's assertion that an asset performs well.

That distinction supports the broader PBS goals of transparency, auditability, and evidence-based evaluation.

Agricultural Example

How does SeedBid™ illustrate the Performance Oracle concept?

SeedBid™ is the proposed agricultural implementation of the PBS/MPS architecture.

In the agricultural example, the Provenance Ledger and Performance Oracle serve different purposes. Provenance addresses the documented history of the agricultural genetic asset and its contributions. Performance addresses real-world results associated with the asset.

This separation illustrates why PBS/MPS treats provenance and performance as distinct layers rather than combining them into a single assertion of value.

Why does performance verification matter to policymakers?

The proposed PBS framework depends on more than documenting intangible assets. It also proposes that relevant real-world performance information be capable of independent examination.

For policymakers, this makes performance verification part of the larger discussion about transparency, investor protection, auditability, and oversight of proposed provenance-backed markets.

The proposed Washington PBS legislation includes performance oracles among the entities contemplated within its registration and oversight framework.

Public Architecture

What does this page publish?

It is not an oracle implementation specification, valuation methodology, investment model, data-feed specification, or securities offering document.

Its purpose is to explain the concept clearly enough for legislators, policymakers, researchers, and the public to understand the role of independently observable performance information within the proposed framework.
