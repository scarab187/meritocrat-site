Washington DFI Regulatory History of PBS/MPS

A factual account of ScarabCloud Inc.'s engagement with the Washington State Department of Financial Institutions concerning Provenance-Backed Securities and the Meritocratic Provenance System.

Important Context

The Washington State Department of Financial Institutions (DFI) has not endorsed ScarabCloud Inc., PBS/MPS, SeedBid™, Meritocrat.org, or the proposed Washington Provenance-Backed Securities Act.

DFI did not issue a classification ruling determining whether PBS is or is not a security, and DFI did not provide ScarabCloud with legal or financial advice.

Why publish this regulatory history?

ScarabCloud engaged DFI during the early development of PBS/MPS to better understand how existing Washington securities laws might apply to the proposed architecture.

This page exists to preserve a clear distinction between what ScarabCloud proposed, what DFI actually communicated, and what policy conclusions ScarabCloud subsequently reached.

The objective is transparency. DFI's statements should be evaluated as DFI made them, without implying endorsement, approval, or a classification decision that DFI did not provide.
