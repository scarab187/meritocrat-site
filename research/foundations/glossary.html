Glossary

Core terms used across the Meritocrat.org research network.

Meritocratic Provenance System (MPS)
Proposed institutional framework for tracing, assessing, and correcting non-meritocratic inputs while preserving market information and incentives.
Provenance
The traceable causal history of an economic outcome or contribution.
Provenance Auditing
Proposed assessment of causal inputs to an economic outcome, expressed probabilistically rather than as perfect reconstruction.
Advantage Index
Proposed estimate of the proportion of an economic return attributed to non-meritocratic inputs.
Provenance-Backed Securities (PBS)
Proposed instrument layer linking economic claims to provenance-verified contributions or assets.
Provenance Ledger
Proposed record layer for contribution, identity, timestamp, evidence, and context.
Performance Oracle
Proposed measurement layer for domain-specific performance evidence.
Contribution Decomposition
Proposed process for estimating the marginal, interacting, and time-dependent value of contributions.
Persistence
The modeled continuation of a contribution’s influence over time.
Synergy
Additional modeled value associated with contributions acting together rather than separately.
Epistemic Humility
Design principle requiring uncertainty in provenance estimates to be acknowledged and reflected in the strength of resulting action.
