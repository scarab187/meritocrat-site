Meritocrat.org Research · Foundations Series

Foundations for an economy that remembers contribution.

A disciplined research sequence connecting the public vision of Meritocrat.org to the evidentiary, economic, institutional, and procedural work required before contribution records can support lawful consequences.

Explore the seriesView the analytical sequence

Purpose of the series

Principles must be able to become institutions.

The Foundations Series begins before ownership, payment, and policy. It asks what must be known, measured, tested, governed, and contestable before contribution evidence becomes consequential.

The sequence moves from economic memory to institutional economics, then to public legitimacy and the rights of the recorded contributor. The objective is reciprocal rather than adversarial: preserve the coordinating role of enterprise while giving contributors a credible, portable, bounded, and contestable evidentiary position.

Work and capital need not be treated as opposing forces when each depends upon the other. The stronger compact begins with evidence, proceeds through independent warrants, and remains bounded by dignity and procedural rights.

Canonical analytical sequence

No consequence without the steps that justify it.

Each stage answers a different institutional question. No stage automatically becomes the next, and the sequence may stop when the evidence cannot support a stronger conclusion.

1

Provenance

What happened, who contributed, in which context, and what evidence supports the record?

2

Performance

What observable outcome occurred under domain-valid measures, baselines, and comparison conditions?

3

Attribution / Allocation

How do documented inputs relate to outcomes under disclosed assumptions, interactions, counterfactual limits, and uncertainty?

4

Entitlement / Payment

What independent legal, contractual, moral, regulatory, or governance rule creates an enforceable consequence?

Foundations Series

Four completed foundations, followed by evidence-building and legal application.

The completed papers are designed to be read in sequence, but each page also stands alone. The planned positions remain visibly separated from completed research so future evidence and legal claims are not presented prematurely.

Why Markets Record Possession Better Than Contribution

Possession is comparatively compact to record. Contribution is a costly causal history. This paper asks when portable contribution evidence improves coordination enough to justify the full cost of recording, validation, attribution, correction, privacy, governance, and administration.

What holds the series together

Memory without automatic entitlement. Measurement without human ranking. Innovation with procedural restraint.

Evidence first

A contribution record preserves relevant history. It does not manufacture ownership, payment, or moral desert.

Economics included

Recording is warranted only where the value of better information exceeds the full institutional cost of preserving and governing it.

Rights throughout

Notice, access, correction, privacy, contestability, appeal, exit, and anti-retaliation follow the record through every consequential stage.
